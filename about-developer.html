Why Talken exists

Chat should feel owned, not rented.

Most messaging apps hide the interesting parts behind company servers. Talken is the opposite idea — the pieces that matter belong to you.

No central chat server

Messages go person to person. That’s the heart of the whole project.

Your own storage

Photos and files live in your Google Drive, not in a company vault.

Your key, your account

No phone number, no contact-list upload. A key only you hold.

Hand-built, end to end

The apps, the networking, identity, updates, recovery — built piece by piece, with intent.

The road here
