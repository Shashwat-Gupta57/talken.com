Why I built Talken

I wanted chat to feel owned, not rented.

Most messaging products hide the interesting parts behind corporate infrastructure. Talken became my attempt to build the opposite: a desktop app where identity, routing, storage, and control are understandable pieces of the product.

No central chat serverPeer-to-peer routing as the product's emotional core.
Your own storageMedia and files tied to the user's cloud, not a company vault.
Hand-built systemsJavaFX UI, networking, identity, updates, recovery, and packaging built with intent.
The flagship

Talken is the proof of taste.

It is not just a school project page. It is a working desktop product with deep technical roots and a visual identity built around private infrastructure.
