What you get

Privacy is just the start.

Really big files

Movies, whole albums, project folders — up to your Drive’s space, not a tiny attachment cap.

Personas

Show one name and photo to work, another to friends. Each contact sees the you that you choose.

Public channels

One person posts, anyone can follow — like a tiny newspaper. Anyone can read, even without an account.

Calls and voice notes

Voice calls and quick voice messages, over the same private route as everything else.

The important stuff

Four things, kept short.

01

No middleman

Messages travel from your device to theirs. There isn’t a company server in between to read, store, or lose them.

02

Big files, no shrinking

Photos, videos and files ride through your own Google Drive — so a 4 GB video sends as easily as a text, at full quality.

Free, with nothing to sell
