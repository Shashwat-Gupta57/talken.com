The problem

Most chat apps ask you to trust the middle.

Messages, files, metadata, accounts, backups - everything tends to orbit one company's servers. Talken starts from a different instinct: remove the middle wherever possible.

01Server receives

Your message passes through someone else's infrastructure.

The Talken idea

Make privacy feel architectural.

Talken is designed as a set of user-owned paths: direct peer routes, user cloud media, local identity, and recovery controls that keep the account understandable.

How it moves

P2P mesh routing

Two users connect over a Yggdrasil-style IPv6 mesh. The server helps with discovery, but the conversation itself is shaped as a direct route.
